ABOUT THE EPISODE

For Michael Sidler, Founding Partner at Redalpine, today's venture landscape is unlike anything he's seen in 20 years.

Here's why this could be Europe's big moment:

  • Valuation gap. Public markets are at highs, private markets are still cheap.
  • IPO window. Klarna's NYSE debut shows belief is back.
  • Capital flows. Rates are falling, liquidity is returning to venture.
  • Crisis vintage effect. The toughest years often create the best vintages.
  • Deep tech edge. Europe's strength lies where software meets engineering.
  • Democratization. Redalpine's Summit Fund opens VC access through new evergreen structures.

Venture in Europe is no longer just about startups, it's becoming culture. Founders are rock stars, LPs are stepping in, and the asset class is maturing fast.
