ABOUT THE EPISODE

Rohit Yadav sits down with Michael Motschmann, Founder & Managing Partner at MIG Capital, a €1B+ venture platform based in Munich with one of the most unique LP bases in Europe: everyday retail investors. From backing BioNTech at inception to building Fund 18, Michael has pioneered a people-first venture model, well before "democratization" became a buzzword.

We unpack:

  • The founding of BioNTech, and how MIG helped shape one of the most impactful companies in medical history.
  • Why MIG raises venture capital from retail investors, not institutions.
  • How Germany's banking DNA shaped its late start in VC.
  • What Europe needs to compete globally: capital market reform, defense tech, and homegrown IPOs.
  • How deep tech, from semiconductors to oncology, will anchor Europe's next growth wave.
  • Why AI is a tool, not a substitute, for founders, scientists, or fund managers.

We also dive into:

  • Why family offices often understand risk better than institutions.
  • The "hidden potential" of Germany's scientific ecosystem.
  • How capital flows need to change to support European strategic autonomy.
  • Why now is the right time to enter venture, despite macro uncertainty.
  • What every investor can learn from MIG's 20-year journey through cycles, exits, and conviction.
