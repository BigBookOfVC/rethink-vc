ABOUT THE EPISODE

Rohit Yadav sits down with Sim Desai, Founder & CEO of Hiive, the fastest-growing platform for VC secondaries. With over $200M in monthly transaction volume and a user base that's transforming how liquidity works in private markets, Hiive is building the Nasdaq for venture-backed startups, before they ever go public.

We explore:

  • The wild history of secondaries, from early Facebook trades to today's unicorn bottlenecks.
  • Why founders restricting employee liquidity may be shooting themselves in the foot.
  • The difference between GP-led, LP-led, and company secondaries (and why the last one is exploding).
  • Bid-ask spreads, illiquidity discounts, and how tech is narrowing the gap.
  • How transparency is the unlock for both premium valuations and happier employees.
  • Hiive's "Hiive 50" index, and what it says about the future of private market pricing.

We also talk about:

  • The role of brokers vs. tech platforms (and why they'll co-exist).
  • Why VCs are converting into RIAs to play in this market.
  • The rise of model disclosures, 1-page KPI reports that drive liquidity without revealing your "secret sauce".
  • What the opening of the IPO window could mean for the next wave of venture liquidity.

This episode is a must-watch if you're a founder, operator, investor, or just curious about the invisible engine behind the modern venture ecosystem.
