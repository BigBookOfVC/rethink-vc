ABOUT THE EPISODE

Rohit Yadav sits down with Joel Sandhu, Partner and Co-Founder at Investors Capital, who co-founded TopTier Access, a fund-of-funds manager backed by leading Belgian family offices. With a clear shift away from venture capital into disciplined, mid-market buyouts, Joel shares how TopTier Access is designing a smarter, more durable playbook for private market exposure.

From frothy VC dreams to dependable buyout outcomes, this is an insider's guide to how LPs are thinking today.

You'll learn:

  • Why venture capital's risk-return tradeoff doesn't always make sense for family offices.
  • How TopTier Access structures vintage-diversified portfolios for optimal resilience.
  • Why the obsession with "replicability" defines their fund manager selection.
  • The mid-market gold rush, and why mega-cap buyout funds may be past their prime.
  • The real story behind co-investments, and why most fund-of-funds are chasing the wrong alpha.
  • How Joel screens for quality GPs using everything from MOIC distributions to C-suite interviews.

We also unpack:

  • The shifting psychology of family offices across Europe and the Middle East.
  • Why most co-investment rights aren't worth the paper they're written on.
  • How returns, liquidity and tax efficiency are shaping next-gen private capital platforms.
  • The one word that defines Joel's fund diligence: replicability.

If you're navigating private markets, this episode is your masterclass in thinking long-term, acting programmatically, and managing risk with surgical precision.
