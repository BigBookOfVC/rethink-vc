# Rethinking Venture Capital

A strategic lens on the venture asset class, across its whole value chain.

Report first published in May 2025.

Read in 50+ countries and downloaded by LPs of all shapes and sizes: sovereign
entities, institutional investors like pension funds, banks, wealth managers,
family offices, fund-of-funds, and angel investors.

[rethink.bigbook.vc](https://rethink.bigbook.vc)

## The Report

A 360-degree view of venture, from LP capital flows through fund mechanics to
exits, with the themes traced end to end rather than in isolation.

## Interviews

Conversations with the LPs, GPs, allocators and operators behind the report,
each with chapters, the figures they quoted, and an index of the companies,
people and data mentioned.
